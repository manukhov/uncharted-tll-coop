**Not-alive branch** (`vtable+2144` false):

**Implication:** for a visually alive puppet, `422760` runs **only** when
`player+0x15A9` (+5545) **bit1** is set. If bit1 is clear, hub entity never gets
bit0 from this path → morning `144610` clears bit4 → `TickDecisionNode` gated off.
